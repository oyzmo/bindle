## GNOME runtime

Bindle is distributed as a Flatpak bundle that runs against the `org.gnome.Platform`
runtime. GTK 4, libadwaita, GLib and the rest of the platform are **not** included
in the bundle: they are supplied by the runtime and linked dynamically at startup.

GTK, libadwaita and GLib are licensed under the GNU Lesser General Public License,
version 2.1 or later. A copy is available at <https://www.gnu.org/licenses/lgpl-2.1.html>
and in the runtime itself. Because these libraries are linked dynamically from a
separately installed runtime, you may replace them with a modified version by
installing a modified `org.gnome.Platform` runtime.
